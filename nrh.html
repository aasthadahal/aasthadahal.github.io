Findings

Neither clears the "1% rule" — and that's normal here

North Richland Hills is a high-value, owner-occupier market: the median home is roughly $476,000 against an average 3-bed rent near $2,590 (~0.54%). Both homes beat that city average, which is precisely what makes them the standouts.

Dream Dust is the newer, lower-maintenance asset

A 2004 build with a 2021 roof means far fewer near-term capital surprises. The tradeoffs are thinner cash flow (slightly negative as modeled) and a weaker crime grade (76180: D+). The motivated seller and recent price cut leave genuine room to negotiate the price down and turn the cash flow positive.

Sunrise is the stronger cash producer in a safer zip

At $250,000 with no HOA, it produces an estimated +$342/month and sits in 76182, which grades a full step safer (C). The tradeoff is age — a 1979 build carries more long-term maintenance risk.

My Recommendation

It comes down to the investor's priority

For monthly income & a safer neighborhood: 6329 Sunrise Dr is the clear winner — positive cash flow from day one, no HOA, and the best rent-to-price ratio available in the city.

For a newer, lower-hassle hold with upside: 6717 Dream Dust Dr — modern build, new roof, and a motivated seller whose price is already moving. Negotiating into the low $300s turns the cash flow positive and secures a 2000s asset that stays easy to maintain and rent for years to come.
