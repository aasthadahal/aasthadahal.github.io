02The one decision that shapes everything

Even at $650k, a 2020+ 2-story doesn't exist in Keller proper. One constraint bends, and each choice leads somewhere different:

Keep Keller ISD → 76244 resale

Six verified 2020-2022 builds with Keller mailing addresses, $469k-$580k, 2,559-3,368 sq ft.

Trade-off: Fort Worth taxes (~2.2%+), smaller lots than old Keller.

Go brand-new → NW corridor or Eagle Mountain

Wellington, Treeline, Talon Hills, Pecan Square: 2-story quick move-ins $475k-$650k with $20k+ incentives and rate buydowns running now.

Trade-off: Northwest / EMS / Argyle schools; some communities carry heavy special-district taxes.

Max value → like-new resale, wider net

Verified 2020+ listings in Haslet, 76131, 76179, Justin, Argyle, Corinth: up to 3,964 sq ft at $490k. Nine have sat 80+ days, which means real negotiating room.

Trade-off: reselling against the builder's next phase in heavy-supply areas.

Before any offer

Pull the parcel's actual taxing entities on TAD.org or dentoncad.com. Special districts are subdivision-specific, not city-wide: two houses a street apart can differ by $4,000/yr. The spread between areas equals roughly $55-70k of purchasing power at current rates.

06Schools & market timing, briefly

  • Keller ISD: TEA rating B. Enrollment down 4,000+ since 2020; the district-split plan was dropped in May 2025, and three intermediate schools close after 2026-27.
  • Northwest ISD: TEA rating B. Fastest-growing district in North Texas; new schools open 2026-27, a fourth high school in 2027. Expect boundary changes in fast-growth pockets.
  • Argyle ISD: TEA rating A (92). Reachable in budget via Retreat at Harvest and select Pecan Square / Roanoke-area homes.
  • Eagle Mountain-Saginaw ISD: serves Talon Hills / 76179 / west-76131; the largest homes per dollar in this search sit here.
  • Market: DFW leans buyer-friendly (~5 months of new-home supply; builders stacking incentives), while Keller-ISD pockets stay tight. Several verified listings have sat 80+ days, so those sellers are negotiable; fresh listings move in days.
